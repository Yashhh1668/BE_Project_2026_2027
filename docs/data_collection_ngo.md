# NGO Data Collection Report

## Overview
As part of our major project work for developing the voice interaction system, field visits were conducted at the NGO to collect primary voice recordings from ID children. These audio samples are crucial for building and training our dataset.

---

## Field Visits & Data Collection Process

* **First Visit:** We initially visited the NGO to collect voice recordings from the children for our dataset. However, after reviewing the collected data, we found that the initial recordings were insufficient in quantity and coverage to adequately train our model.
* **Second Visit:** To address this gap, we conducted a second visit following the exact same process. This ensured we gathered a sufficient and reliable volume of voice recordings required for our project's dataset.

---

## Conclusion
The data collected across both visits successfully provided the necessary voice samples needed to proceed with our dataset preparation and model development.
