# Audio Filtering & Cleaning Workflow

## Tools Used
* **ocenaudio**

---

## Processing Steps

1. **Noise Reduction:**
   * Applying spectral noise suppression to eliminate background ambient noise.

2. **Silence Trimming:**
   * Cutting off leading and trailing silences, as well as extended pauses within each recording.

3. **Audio Normalization:**
   * Adjusting gain and amplitude levels across all audio clips to maintain uniform volume levels.
